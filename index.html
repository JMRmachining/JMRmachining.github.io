What We Do

Our Capabilities

From precision turning to composite fabrication, we offer a comprehensive suite of manufacturing services designed to meet the most demanding specifications.

Lathe Machining

Precision turning and cutting of metal components using state-of-the-art lathes. We handle small-scale through large industrial parts, ensuring tight tolerances and impeccable surface finishes.

Mill Machining

High-accuracy milling for complex geometries. Utilizing advanced milling machines, we produce intricate parts and precise features from a variety of materials to the most demanding specifications.

Mold Making

Custom mold fabrication emphasizing durability and repeatability. Our team designs and builds molds tailored to your production needs, ensuring consistent quality and long tool life.

Molding & Casting

Comprehensive molding and casting services covering a range of techniques for high-quality components — optimized for efficiency and precision, from prototyping to full-scale production.

Composites

Advanced composite services producing lightweight yet robust parts. Ideal for aerospace, defense, and high-performance industries, our composites meet rigorous strength and durability standards.

3D Printing

Cutting-edge 3D printing for detailed prototypes and production parts with rapid turnaround. Supports complex designs and customized components with high resolution and precision.

Sand Blasting

Surface preparation through cleaning, etching, and smoothing. We efficiently remove rust, paint, and imperfections, creating an ideal base for painting, coating, or further processing.

Polishing

Expert polishing services to achieve flawless surface finishes on a variety of materials, enhancing both the aesthetic appeal and functional performance of your components.

Passivation

We provide passivation treatments to enhance the corrosion resistance of stainless steel and other alloys. Our process removes free iron and surface contaminants, extending the life and performance of your components in demanding environments.

Chem Film Treatment

Our chemical film (Alodine/chromate conversion coating) treatments provide excellent corrosion protection and electrical conductivity for aluminum parts. Ideal for aerospace and defense applications requiring MIL-DTL-5541 compliance.
